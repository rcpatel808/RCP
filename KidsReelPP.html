1

Information We Collect

To provide our AI video generation services, we collect minimal necessary information:

  • Account Data: Name, email address, or authentication token (Google/Apple login).
  • User Media: Photos and video clips you upload for AI face swapping and video creation.
  • Technical Metadata: Device model, OS version, app performance logs, and masked IP address.
  • Purchase Info: In-app subscription status managed securely via Google Play / Apple App Store.
2

How We Use Your Data

We process your data strictly for legitimate operational purposes:

  • Rendering face-swapped and animated video reels requested by you.
  • Authenticating your account and maintaining user session security.
  • Managing active subscriptions, credits, and features inside the App.
  • Analyzing crash reports and diagnostic performance to resolve bugs.
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations and preventing fraudulent activity.
3

AI Face Swap & Facial Data Protection

Facial features from uploaded images are analyzed temporarily strictly to map expressions and features onto selected video templates.

  • No Biometric Database: We do NOT create persistent biometric profiles or facial recognition databases.
  • No Shared Model Training: Your facial images are NEVER used to train, fine-tune, or improve public AI models.
  • Strict Confidentiality: Uploaded media is processed through secure, private rendering pipelines.
⚠️ Responsible Image Upload & Consent
You confirm that you own or have explicit legal authorization for any photo uploaded to Kid Reel AI. When uploading photos of minors, you represent that you are the child's parent or legal guardian, or have received explicit consent from the legal guardian.
4

Data Retention & Auto-Cleanup

We retain personal data only for the minimum period necessary to deliver our services:

  • Source images uploaded for processing are automatically purged from active processing cache after generation.
  • Generated video files are stored locally on your device or in your private account history until you delete them.
  • Account credentials are saved securely until account deletion is requested.
5

Data Sharing & Third-Party Partners

We never sell, rent, or monetize your personal images or private data. Limited data is shared only with trusted infrastructure providers under strict privacy agreements:

  • Cloud Compute & Storage: Secure cloud servers (e.g., AWS, GCP) used solely to execute video rendering.
  • App Stores & Billing: Google Play Store and Apple App Store for processing subscriptions and payments.
  • Analytics & Ads: Privacy-compliant diagnostic providers (e.g., Firebase, AdMob) using anonymized identifiers.
6

Security Infrastructure

We employ robust administrative, technical, and physical safeguards including SSL/TLS encryption in transit and AES encryption at rest. While we utilize industry-standard protection, no internet system is 100% immune, and we encourage safe personal password practices.

7

Children's Privacy (COPPA & Family Safety)

Kid Reel AI is designed for adult parents, guardians, and family members (18+) to create fun video memories. We do not knowingly collect personal registration data directly from children under 18.

👨‍👩‍👦 Parental Request Channel
If a parent or guardian believes their child has submitted personal information directly without consent, please contact us immediately for instant deletion.
8

Your Rights & Choices (GDPR, DPDP Act)

Under international privacy laws (GDPR, India's DPDP Act, and state privacy acts), you hold the right to:

  • Access & Export: Request a copy of personal data associated with your account.
  • Rectification & Erasure: Request correction or complete deletion of your account and data.
  • Withdraw Consent: Revoke photo processing permissions at any time.
9

Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect new features or legal requirements. Material updates will be highlighted inside the App or communicated via email.
