Color Ball Jump

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

Thank you for playing Color Ball Jump. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and processed when you download, install, or use the Color Ball Jump mobile application.

Color Ball Jump is an arcade action mobile game where players tap to navigate a colored ball through multi-colored obstacles, matching the ball’s color with the corresponding obstacle color. The game includes several single-player gameplay modes, including Campaign Levels and Daily Challenges.

By downloading, installing, or playing Color Ball Jump, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect

Color Ball Jump does not require you to provide sensitive personal information to enjoy gameplay, such as:

  • Your phone number
  • Your physical home address
  • Your personal email address for standard gameplay
  • Financial or credit card details directly to the game developer

However, third-party services, analytics tools, and platform integrations embedded in the game may automatically collect technical, diagnostic, and gameplay-related data necessary to run, improve, and secure the game.

Automatically Collected Information

Depending on your device, operating system, permissions, and features enabled, collected information may include:

  • Device model, manufacturer, and operating system version
  • Language preferences and approximate country or region
  • Crash reports, diagnostic logs, and frame performance metrics
  • Gameplay statistics (such as high score, stars collected, total attempts, and completed levels)
  • Game progress, unlocked skins, trails, power-ups, themes, and achievements
  • Advertising identifiers, where advertising services are enabled and permitted by your device settings
  • Account or player identifiers associated with platform gaming services, when connected for leaderboards or achievements

How We Use Information

Information processed by Color Ball Jump and its supporting services is used to:

  • Provide, maintain, and run the core gameplay features
  • Save, restore, and display your game progress, high scores, stars, and unlocked customizations
  • Support platform gaming services, including leaderboards and achievement tracking
  • Identify, troubleshoot, and fix bugs, crashes, and performance issues
  • Improve physics responsiveness, gameplay balance, and user experience
  • Deliver updates, new levels, themes, and game enhancements
  • Display and measure advertisements, where advertising is enabled

Gameplay Data & Local Storage

Your game progress, scores, stars, custom skins, and sound preferences are primarily saved locally on your device. Where supported, game progress and achievements may also be backed up or synchronized through cloud storage or platform gaming services.

If you play without an internet connection or without connecting a platform gaming account, your progress remains stored locally on your device.

Platform Gaming Services

Color Ball Jump may support integration with your device's platform gaming services for features such as global leaderboards, achievements, and cloud saves. If you choose to sign in to a platform gaming account, the platform provider processes your data in accordance with its own privacy policy and your account preferences. We do not control your platform provider’s independent data practices.

Cloud Services & Diagnostics

To support cloud data backups, game analytics, and technical crash reporting, Color Ball Jump may use cloud-based infrastructure and analytics frameworks. These services process pseudonymous technical information and gameplay telemetry to ensure game stability, prevent data loss, and resolve technical issues.

Advertising

If advertising is enabled in your version of Color Ball Jump, third-party advertising partners may process technical device information and advertising identifiers to display, optimize, and measure non-personalized or personalized advertisements.

You can adjust, reset, or opt out of interest-based ad tracking at any time through the privacy settings on your device.

In-App Purchases

If Color Ball Jump offers optional in-app purchases (such as ad removal, star packs, or cosmetic skins), all financial transactions are processed securely through the applicable app store where you downloaded the game. We never receive, store, or have access to your payment card details or banking information.

The app store provides the game with transaction receipts to verify purchases and restore eligible content.

Third-Party Services

Color Ball Jump may utilize trusted third-party providers for essential game operations, including analytics, crash reporting, ad serving, and platform game services. These providers collect and process data subject to their respective privacy policies.

Children’s Privacy

Color Ball Jump is designed as an arcade game for general audiences. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information directly from children under the age required by applicable local law.

If you believe personal information of a child has been inadvertently collected through third-party services, please contact us so we can investigate and remove the data promptly.

Data Security

We take reasonable administrative and technical precautions to safeguard the integrity of game data. However, please be aware that no electronic storage method or transmission over the internet can be guaranteed 100% secure.

Data Retention & Deletion

We retain data only as long as reasonably required to provide the features and services described in this Privacy Policy.

  • Local Data: You can completely clear locally stored game data and settings by clearing the application storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the game.
  • Cloud Data: If you wish to request the deletion of any cloud-associated gameplay data under our control, please submit a deletion request to our support email

Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights regarding your information under applicable data protection laws, including the right to request access, correction, or deletion of data associated with you.

You may also control advertising preferences and platform service permissions through your device's built-in privacy and application settings.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our game or applicable legal regulations. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date. We encourage players to periodically check this page for updates.

Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or Color Ball Jump: Strategy Board, please contact the developer using the support contact provided on the game's Google Play Store listing.
