Last Updated: Oct 2026

Thank you for playing Memory Chess: Brain Puzzle. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and processed when you download, install, or use the Memory Chess: Brain Puzzle mobile game.

Memory Chess: Brain Puzzle is a color-based memory and puzzle game in which players roll a six-color dice and find the matching color peg on the board. The game provides five ways to play: Play with Computer, Classical Level Mode, Play and Pass, Online Multiplayer, and Friends Rooms.

By downloading or using Memory Chess: Brain Puzzle, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

However, third-party services integrated into the game may automatically process technical, diagnostic, account, gameplay, or service-related information. Online multiplayer features may also require a player identifier or game-session information so that players can connect and play together.

This may include a player or platform identifier, display name or nickname where available, room or game identifier, connection/session information, game state, match result, and other information required to synchronize gameplay between players.

Friends Rooms may use a room code, lobby code, or similar session identifier so that players can join the same game. Players should avoid entering personal, sensitive, or confidential information as a display name, room name, chat field, or other publicly visible gameplay information if such features are provided.

We do not intend for multiplayer functionality to require players to disclose their personal phone number, home address, or other sensitive personal information to other players.

Play and Pass Mode

Play and Pass is a local multiplayer mode in which multiple players can play on the same device. Information used during this mode is primarily processed on the device for gameplay. Players should be aware that anyone with access to the device may be able to see gameplay information stored locally on that device.

Play with Computer and Classical Level Mode are gameplay modes that can be played without connecting to another player. Game progress, scores, levels, and related statistics may be stored locally or synchronized through supported online services when those services are available.
