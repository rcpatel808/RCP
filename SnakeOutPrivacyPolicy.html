Privacy Policy

Last Updated: Aug 2026

Thank you for playing Snake Swipe. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and processed when you download, install, or use the Snake Swipe mobile game.

Snake Swipe is a colorful arrow-swipe puzzle game featuring 1,000 levels, progressively increasing difficulty, boosters such as Hint, Grid, and Hammer, and Google Play Games Services features including leaderboards and achievements.

By downloading or using Snake Swipe, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect

Snake Swipe does not directly ask you to provide personal information such as:

  • Your name
  • Phone number
  • Home address
  • Payment information

However, third-party services integrated into the game may automatically process technical, diagnostic, gameplay, or service-related information.

Automatically Collected Information

Depending on your device, operating system, permissions, and the services you use, information may include:

  • Device model and operating system version
  • Language and country or region
  • Crash reports and performance diagnostics
  • Anonymous game usage and gameplay statistics
  • Advertising identifiers, if advertising services are enabled
  • Information associated with Google Play Games Services when you use leaderboards or achievements

How We Use Information

  • Provide and maintain the game
  • Save and display gameplay-related progress and achievements where applicable
  • Provide Google Play Games Services features such as leaderboards and achievements
  • Improve game performance and user experience
  • Identify and fix bugs, crashes, and technical problems
  • Maintain security and prevent abuse
  • Deliver updates and new features
  • Display and measure advertisements, if advertising is enabled

Google Play Games Services

Snake Swipe uses Google Play Games Services for features such as leaderboards and achievements. If you sign in to Google Play Games, Google may process information according to Google's applicable policies and your Google account settings. We do not control Google's data processing practices.

Please review Google's privacy information for details about how Google processes information.

Advertising

If advertising is enabled in Snake Swipe, third-party advertising providers such as Google AdMob may process advertising identifiers and related information to provide, personalize where permitted, and measure advertisements.

If advertising is not enabled in the version of Snake Swipe you are using, this section does not apply to your use of the game.

Snake Swipe may use third-party services required for game functionality, analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or platform features. These may include Google Play Games Services and, if enabled, Google AdMob or other Google services.

Third-party providers may collect and process information under their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review the applicable policies of those providers.

If you have a privacy or data-deletion request relating to information that we control, please contact us using the contact details published with the game. Requests involving Google Play Games or other third-party services may need to be handled directly through those services.
